We are delighted to welcome Krystyna Piela to the Williams Lab as a postdoctoral researcher! Krystyna earned her PhD at the University of Glasgow, where she studied oral epithelial barrier integrity and neutrophil responses to oral biofilms. Her research in the lab will explore how interactions between epithelial and stromal cells influence inflammatory responses in periodontal disease. Welcome, Krystyna!
