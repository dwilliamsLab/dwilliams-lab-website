Drake received a 2026 Grant for Junior Faculty from the American Federation for Aging Research (AFAR), joining eight early-career investigators selected for support to study the biology of aging.

The award will support our research on the spatial dynamics of microbiome-induced inflammaging in barrier tissue, examining how interactions between microbes and the host contribute to age-associated inflammation.

Read more in [Drake's AFAR grantee profile](https://www.afar.org/grantee-profiles/drake-williams-dds-phd), the [AFAR recipient announcement](https://www.afar.org/news/announcing-2026-afar-grants-for-junior-faculty-recipients), and [additional coverage](https://bioengineer.org/afar-funds-eight-junior-faculty-projects-on-mechanisms-of-aging/).
