Krystyna is a postdoctoral researcher in the Williams Lab at the University of Minnesota. Her research interests include oral mucosal immunology, host–microbe interactions, and links between oral and systemic health. She holds a PhD from the University of Glasgow, where she developed a three-dimensional co-culture model of the oral mucosal barrier to investigate epithelial barrier integrity and neutrophil responses to oral biofilms. She is a registered dentist in Poland and the UK. Her current research focuses on how interactions between epithelial and stromal cells influence inflammatory responses in periodontal disease. Outside the lab, she enjoys traveling and learning languages.
